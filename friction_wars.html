Make recovery
the easy
option.

At every decision point, people do whatever takes less effort at that moment. In this exercise you change Sam's environment so that the recovery options take less effort than the stressful ones, then watch what Sam does over a fortnight.

How will the room play?

Two teams

Each team designs Sam's environment with the same budget. The team whose Sam recovers more often wins.

One design for the whole room

The room agrees on a single design together.

The effort values, card costs and events are set for teaching. They are not measurements, and the exercise does not use anyone's HRV data.

per team, to spend on changes to Sam's environment. Some changes alter the effort at the moment Sam chooses. Others do not.

Sam's fortnight: two decisions a day

Each team gets the same budget and the same list of cards, shown when you start designing. The cards are not labelled and the budget will not cover everything that works. Your task is to decide which cards change the effort at 13:00 and 22:30, on the days that matter.
Design

Spend the budget on Sam's environment

The facilitator enters each team's choices. Cards you cannot afford are greyed out. Before running, write down how often you expect Sam to recover.

Questions for the room

    There is no chance in the model: every team faces the same fortnight, so every difference between teams comes from their cards. Habit strength rises each time an option is chosen, faster when it is rewarding, and fades a little when it is not. A second card on the same option counts for 72% of its value, a third for 52%.
